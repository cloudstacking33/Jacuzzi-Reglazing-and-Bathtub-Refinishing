Reglazing Versus Replacing: The Smart Choice in New York

In a city where every square foot counts, tearing out a bathtub is rarely practical. Access through narrow hallways, building rules, shared plumbing and neighbors below you all make replacement slow and stressful. Bathtub reglazing avoids nearly all of it. A typical reglazing job starts at a fraction of the cost of replacement, with average starting prices of roughly $350 for reglazing compared with about $3,000 for a new tub, before any tile or wall repair is added.

Beyond cost, reglazing is gentler on your home and on the environment. Nothing heavy leaves the building, no walls are opened and no tile has to be matched or reinstalled. Most bathtub and tile reglazing projects are finished in about two to four hours, and you can usually plan to use the surface again after the cure time we explain on the day of service. With proper care and non abrasive cleaners, a professionally reglazed surface can serve you well for many years.

Bathtub reglazing brings worn, dull or stained tubs back to a clean, glossy white or a color of your choice. Fiberglass Reglazing and Fiberglass Repair

Fiberglass tubs and showers are lightweight and common in New York apartments, but their surfaces can yellow, scratch and develop stress cracks.
